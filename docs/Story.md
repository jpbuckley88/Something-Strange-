# Story

## Premise

Ned is a teenage genius. One of his experiments tears open the spirit realm and releases ghosts and demons into the world. His mom is caught and dragged back through. Ned has to remove the ghosts and demons and rescue her.

## Characters

- **Ned.** The player character. Brilliant, impatient, and responsible for all of this. Always seen in first person.
- **Mom.** Taken in the opening minutes. Her screen time is short, so her presence in the house (notes, photos, her voice on the answering machine) has to carry the relationship.
- **Whatever took her.** Not named or seen clearly until the end.

## Outline

| # | Level | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The House | The experiment fails in Ned's basement lab. A rift opens, things pour out, and Mom is taken. Ned grabs the prototype laser and goggles, clears the house, and seals the rift, too late to follow her. |
| 2 | The Neighbourhood | The entities have spread. Ned learns that each sealed rift gives him a reading on where Mom is. |
| 3 | The School | The first demon. The laser alone is not enough, so Ned upgrades in the lab. |
| 4 | The Town | One or two levels, for example the hospital and the power station that fed the experiment. |
| 5 | The Spirit Realm | Ned rebuilds the machine on purpose and goes through. |
| 6 | The Rescue | Final confrontation with whatever took her, then closing the door behind them. |

Level count is a scope dial. The vertical slice is Level 1. A small complete game is levels 1, 2, 5, and 6.

## Level 1: The House

Rooms: basement lab, garage, kitchen, living room, hallway and stairs, Ned's bedroom, Mom's bedroom, bathroom, attic.

1. **Prologue and tutorial.** Ned walks home from school, goes down to the basement lab, and finishes building the goggles and the laser. Testing them is the tutorial. The full script is in [Script.md](Script.md).
2. **The accident.** Ned starts a second experiment, a window into the spirit realm. It does not close. Mom is taken. The power goes out.
3. **Unarmed.** Ned has the goggles, but the surge burst the laser's power cell. He has to reach the spare in the garage, able to see what is in the house and unable to hurt it.
4. **Clearing the house.** Kitchen, living room, upstairs, attic. Each area introduces one entity type: wisps, then a poltergeist, then a shade.
5. **Return to the lab.** Restore the power, go back down, fight the rift demon, destroy the anchors, and seal the rift.
6. **Ending.** The rift is closed, Mom is not there, and the street outside is not quiet.
