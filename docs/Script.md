# Script: Prologue

The opening of the game, from Ned walking home to the moment Level 1 begins. It runs about 15 minutes and contains the whole tutorial. Nothing in it can hurt Ned until the last scene.

First draft. Dialogue is there to be rewritten; the beats and the order of the tutorial are what matter.

## What the prologue has to do

- Teach every control before anything is trying to kill Ned.
- Make the player like Mom in very little screen time.
- Show the house in daylight, so the player knows its layout before it goes dark.
- Explain why a teenager owns goggles that see ghosts and a laser that hurts them.

## Tutorial at a glance

| Scene | Teaches | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The walk home | Look, move | Walk up the street to the house |
| 2. Home | Interact, doors | Front door, the note, the fridge |
| 3. The lab | Picking things up | Collect three parts for the goggles |
| 4. The goggles | Goggles, charge, recharge | Look at the specimen jar |
| 5. The emitter | Fire, heat, overheating | Burn a test target |
| 6. Calibration | Frequencies, matched damage | Meat with one frequency, the specimen with the other |
| 7. The aperture | (story) | Start the experiment |

Each instruction appears once, as a short line on screen, when the player first needs it. Ned's own lines carry the rest.

## Scene 1: The walk home

*Exterior. A quiet suburban street, late afternoon, autumn. Low sun, long shadows. NED, fifteen, walks home from school with a backpack. First person throughout: we never see his face.*

*His phone buzzes. A text from MOM on screen:*

> **MOM:** Working till 6. Lasagna tonight. Do NOT start anything in the basement until I'm home.

> **MOM:** I mean it. Last time the Hendersons' garage door opened by itself for a week.

**ON SCREEN:** *Move the mouse to look. W A S D to walk.*

*Ned walks. A neighbour drags a bin to the kerb and waves. A dog behind a fence barks at him, then, as he passes, turns and barks at the empty air beside him.*

**NED:** *(muttering)* Yeah, I know. I felt it too.

*He reaches the house: two storeys, a garage, a basement window at ground level glowing faintly blue.*

**NED:** Left it running. Good. That's good. That's eight hours of data.

> **Scope note.** The street is the only outdoor area before Level 2, and Level 2 reuses it. If it has to be cut, start at the front door and deliver the texts there.

## Scene 2: Home

**ON SCREEN:** *E to interact.*

*Ned opens the front door. The house is warm, lived-in, a little untidy. Family photos in the hall: Ned and Mom, just the two of them, at different ages.*

*On the kitchen counter, a note in Mom's handwriting, weighed down with an apple.*

> *Eat something that isn't cereal. Love you. — M*

*The player can explore freely. Every room is open and lit. Things worth finding:*

- *The fridge: the lasagna, wrapped, with a second note: "For TONIGHT. Hands off."*
- *The freezer: a pork shoulder. Ned takes it.*

**NED:** Sorry, Mom. Science.

- *Mom's bedroom: a nurse's uniform on the door. A framed newspaper clipping: LOCAL BOY, 12, WINS STATE SCIENCE FAIR.*
- *Ned's bedroom: whiteboards covered in equations, one phrase circled three times: "NOT GONE. JUST OUT OF PHASE."*
- *The basement door, in the hall, with a hand-drawn sign: KNOCK FIRST. SERIOUSLY.*

*Ned goes down.*

## Scene 3: The lab

*The basement. Half of it is a family basement: washing machine, boxes, an old sofa. The other half is Ned's: workbenches, salvaged electronics, cables stapled along the joists. At the far wall, under a tarpaulin, something large.*

*On the main bench: a sealed glass jar wired to a car battery, apparently empty. A monitor beside it shows a waveform, steady.*

**NED:** Still there. Eight hours. You're real, you're stable, and I can't see you. Yet.

*He drops the pork shoulder on the bench. On a stand: a pair of welding goggles, gutted and rewired, three empty sockets.*

**NED:** Lens array. Tuning coil. Power cell. Where did I...

**OBJECTIVE:** *Find the three missing parts.*

*The parts are around the basement: the lens array on a shelf, the coil inside the gutted microwave, the power cell on charge by the washing machine. Collecting each teaches picking things up and gets the player to walk the whole room.*

*With all three, Ned returns to the bench.*

**ON SCREEN:** *E to assemble.*

*A short sequence of his hands seating each part. The goggles hum.*

## Scene 4: The goggles

**ON SCREEN:** *Right mouse to switch the goggles on.*

*The world shifts: colour drains, edges glow. In the jar, where there was nothing, a mote of pale light turns slowly, like something asleep.*

**NED:** *(barely breathing)* Hi.

*A beat. He laughs, once, astonished.*

**NED:** I was right. I was right. They said it was sensor noise and I was *right*.

*The charge runs out. The goggles cut off. The jar looks empty again.*

**NED:** Five seconds. Okay. Capacitors. That's a tomorrow problem.

**ON SCREEN:** *The goggles recharge while they are off.*

*If the player waits and looks again, the mote has drifted to the side of the jar nearest Ned.*

## Scene 5: The emitter

*On the second bench: a device the size of a cordless drill, all heat sinks and exposed coil, missing its housing.*

**NED:** Seeing it is half. If it's energy on a frequency, I can push energy back at it on the same frequency. Touch it. Say hello properly.

**ON SCREEN:** *E to assemble.*

*He fits the housing. It is the laser.*

**NED:** *(reading the side, where he has written it in marker)* "Resonant emitter, mark four." Marks one to three are why we have a new smoke alarm.

*Across the room, a paint can on a stool.*

**ON SCREEN:** *Hold left mouse to fire.*

*The beam is orange. The can scorches, then bursts. If the player keeps firing, the heat bar fills and the emitter cuts out with a whine.*

**ON SCREEN:** *The emitter overheats. Let it cool.*

**NED:** Don't melt it. Took me three weeks to find that coil.

## Scene 6: Calibration

*Ned props the pork shoulder on the stool.*

**NED:** Two settings. Material: things that are properly here. Spectral: things that aren't. Yet. Let's see what each one does to each.

**OBJECTIVE:** *Test the material frequency on the meat.*

*Orange beam. The meat sears, splits, and burns through to the bone in a second or two. It is the first gore in the game, and it is a pork shoulder.*

**NED:** Okay. So don't point that at anything I like.

**ON SCREEN:** *Q to switch frequency.*

**OBJECTIVE:** *Test the spectral frequency on the meat.*

*Blue beam. Almost nothing: a faint warming.*

**NED:** Nothing. Passes straight through. Good.

**OBJECTIVE:** *Test the spectral frequency on the specimen.*

*The player has to put the goggles on to see the mote, then fire. The blue beam touches it. The mote flares, flattens against the glass, and the waveform on the monitor spikes. The jar cracks.*

*Ned stops.*

**NED:** Sorry. Sorry! Okay. That hurt you. That's... huh.

*He looks at the emitter in his hand.*

**NED:** That's not what I built it for.

*The mote, through the goggles, has backed into the far side of the jar.*

## Scene 7: The aperture

*Upstairs, the front door. Keys in a bowl.*

**MOM:** *(off screen, calling)* Ned? I'm home! Did you eat?

**NED:** *(calling back)* Yes!

**MOM:** *(off)* Something that wasn't cereal?

**NED:** *(a pause)* ...It had fruit in it!

*She laughs. Footsteps cross the kitchen. The oven door.*

*Ned pulls the tarpaulin off the thing at the far wall: a ring of salvaged electromagnets taller than he is, cabled into the house's breaker panel.*

**NED:** One specimen is a fluke. One specimen got in by accident. If I can open a window, a small one, for one second, and look through...

**OBJECTIVE:** *Start the aperture.*

*Three switches, in order. Each one dims the lights in the house. On the third:*

**MOM:** *(off, closer; she's at the top of the stairs)* Ned, the lights are doing the thing again. What did I say about the basement?

**NED:** One second! Literally one second!

*The ring fires. In its centre, the air folds. Through it: somewhere dark, vast, and crowded.*

*Ned puts the goggles on. Through them the window is a hundred times larger than the ring, and everything on the other side has turned to look at it.*

**NED:** Oh.

*He hits the cut-off. Nothing. He hits it again. The ring's cables are glowing.*

**MOM:** *(on the stairs now, coming down, a dish towel over her shoulder)* Ned, what is that sound? Ned...

*She sees it. She does the only thing she would ever do: she crosses the room and puts herself between it and him.*

**MOM:** Get behind me. Get behind me, baby, go, go upstairs...

*An arm comes through. It is the first merged thing we see: too long, raw, wet, with joints in the wrong places, forcing its way into the world with a sound like tearing canvas. It takes her around the waist. She is lifted off the floor.*

**MOM:** NED...

*She is pulled through. Her hand catches the edge of the ring, and for a moment holds. Then it does not.*

*The ring overloads. A white flash. Ned is thrown across the room.*

*Black.*

## Level 1 begins

*Ned comes to on the basement floor. The lights are out. The ring is a ruin, but the fold in the air is still there, wider, held open by points of light only the goggles show.*

*The emitter lies beside him, its power cell burst.*

*Mom's dish towel is on the floor under the ring.*

*Upstairs, something heavy moves across the kitchen.*

**NED:** *(a whisper)* Mom?

**OBJECTIVE:** *Get out of the basement.*

## Design notes

- **Ned starts Level 1 with goggles but no working laser.** The surge burst the emitter's power cell, and the spare is in the garage. That gives a stretch where the player can see what is in the house and cannot hurt it, then the relief of arming up. This replaces the earlier idea of finding both items after the accident. It is my suggestion; if you would rather Ned is armed from the start, cut the burst cell.
- **The specimen in the jar** is the same kind of thing as a wisp. Hurting it in the tutorial is the first hint that these things feel, and gives Ned something to be uneasy about. It could return later.
- **The pork shoulder** teaches matched damage with gore that is funny in daylight. The same burn and split effects are reused on merged flesh for the rest of the game, where they are not funny.
- **Mom's last action is protecting him.** The rescue is the whole game; it has to be earned here.
- **The house in daylight** is the same level as the house in the dark. Build it once, light it twice.
